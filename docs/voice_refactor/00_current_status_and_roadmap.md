**ElevenLabs v4 model migration, 2026-10-15 (working tree):** narration (both tiers), previews and the synth lab now use `eleven_v4`; calls use `eleven_v4_turbo` on `/v1/text-to-dialogue/multi-stream-input`. The existing adapters, jobs, playback and stored voice contracts are retained. Older lab selections resolve to v4 for compatibility; old usage and acceptance records retain their original model IDs. Both models send only stability and similarity, support audio tags, and omit speed/style/speaker boost and SSML. The two narration tiers share one acceptance review. Calls hold a separate dialogue-session limiter slot. Active contexts receive 15-second keepalives; a quiet socket expiry reopens on the next turn without spending the reconnect budget. The existing 120-second default / 150-second maximum app call idle policy is preserved independently of the dialogue context's fixed 20-second expiry.

**Direct provider evidence, 2026-10-15:** authenticated `GET /v1/models` listed v4 and v4 Turbo with 94 languages each. A 23-character English preview returned 15,298 MP3 bytes via v4. The same text streamed 15,090 bytes in eight chunks through the production v4 Turbo multi-context adapter, with zero reconnects. These are provider contract smoke checks, not a deployment or a subjective listening acceptance. Voice Design/Remix remains `eleven_ttv_v3`: the separate design endpoint lists no v4 replacement. Sources: [models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models), [v4 voice settings](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech), [dialogue protocol](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-dialogue/ttd-multi-websocket), [realtime behavior](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/guides/how-to/websockets/realtime-tdd), [Voice Design models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-voice/design).
