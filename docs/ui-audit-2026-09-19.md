## Design rules applied

- Use spacing, typography and dividers to group related content. Reserve an enclosing surface for independently actionable objects, selectable states or overlays. This follows [visual hierarchy](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/), [proximity](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/) and [common-region grouping](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/common-region/).
- Keep long detail secondary: plan limits use native disclosures; navigation has one selected treatment; assistant and media accents retain their established semantics.
- Preserve recognizable controls and visible feedback. Loading, failed fetches and true empty collections have distinct output; retry controls remain available.
- Use actual layout space for touch controls, readable muted text and deliberate modal focus. References: [WCAG target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum), [contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [dialog focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/). These changes do not constitute a full WCAG certification.

## Main corrections

- Flattened passive content across chat, authoring, profile, docs, legal and administration while retaining selection, warning and dialog boundaries.
- Fixed phone overflow and clipped menus, selected-tab visibility, compressed player titles, call-caption overlap, field sizing and section-header spacing.
- Corrected shared icon-button positioning, modal reading focus, creator sticky detection/anchor clearance and mobile card/AI menu placement.
- Standardized failure/loading feedback and removed contradictory empty-state messages after failed requests.
- Added repeatable route and overlay fixtures and documented composition guidance in Storybook.
